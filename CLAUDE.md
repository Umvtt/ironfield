# Ironfield: notes for Claude

Ironfield is a top-down, War Thunder-style browser game, built as one self-contained HTML file.

## Layout
- `web/index.html`: the whole game: HTML, CSS and JS in one file. This is what players load. Keep it a single file with no build step.
- `web/manifest.webmanifest`, `web/icon-192.png`, `web/icon-512.png`: make the site installable ("Add to Home Screen" / the hangar's **Install on this device** button). The website is the main way to play; the Android app was retired (old installs still load the site and get game updates via `version.json`, but release tags are `game-<build>` so they never see an APK prompt).
- `.github/workflows/publish-game.yml`: deploys `web/` to GitHub Pages on every push that touches `web/**`, after stamping `<meta name="ironfield-version" content="">` with the build number (`git rev-list --count HEAD -- web`, shown as "Build N" in the hangar's corner; "Build dev" when unstamped), adding `version.json` = first 12 hex chars of `sha256(web/index.html)` of the stamped file plus the commit message's first line as `notes` (shown to players as "What's new").
- `.github/workflows/release.yml` (workflow name "Release"): on pushes to `web/**`, or by hand, makes a release tagged `game-<build>` with `ironfield.html`: `web/index.html` with the `<meta name="ironfield-build" content="">` placeholder stamped with the version, site and download link; a small script at the end of the file then shows an update banner on PC when `version.json` differs. Keep that placeholder and the `ironfield-version` one exactly as is; both workflows stamp the build number the same way before hashing.

## Game systems (all in web/index.html)
- **Damage model:** War Thunder-style. There are no hit points. Crew and modules (engine, transmission, fuel, ammo rack, breech, turret drive) get hit. Shells check armor along their path, then apply spall and filler. The ammo types are AP, APCBC, APHE, HVAP, APDS, APFSDS, HEAT, HESH and HE.
- **Vehicles:** `ROSTER` is built with `V(nation, class, tier, id, name, stats)` for ground vehicles and `A(...)` for aircraft. Nations are usa, ger, ussr, uk and fra. Ground classes are light, medium, heavy, td, spg and aa; air classes are fighter and bomber.
- **Aircraft:** planes sit in the same `tanks` array with `t.air` set. Flight is `updPlane` (turn rate × `planeAgility`: 2.2× for fighters you fly, 1.7× AI ally fighters, 1.15× enemy fighters), AI is `planeAI`, weapons are bullets, rockets and bombs (`updAirShells`), and damage is `planeHit` / `planePost`. Tank guns only hit planes with `alt < .45`. Player aircraft are deliberately forgiving: 0.65× damage taken, slow self-repair to 80 % after 6 s unhit, 1.2× turn rate (bombers; fighters see planeAgility), slower gun heat, shorter fires. AA flak uses a proximity fuze (`T.fz`). AA vehicles are capped to thin armor and fire a weakened copy of their round at ground targets (`T.gnd`: 40 % pen, quarter filler).
- **Map features:** each side has an airfield (`fields`, from `genMap`): planes start over ours; G lowers the landing gear (`toggleGear`, 0.78× top speed while down), then S (touch: GEAR → LAND) over the runway lands — never without gear, and after 4 s on the ground are repaired and rearmed (`toggleLand`, `t.landing/landed/serviced`). The enemy airfield's two hangars (`o.hangar==='e'`) launch enemy aircraft; destroy both and they fly in from far away. Fuel dumps (`kind:'fuel'`) explode when hit (queued in `fuelQ`). Supply depots (`depots`) repair and restock a ground vehicle that holds still in them for 3 s, every 40 s. Player aircraft may fly off the map; more than 500 px out starts a 30 s countdown (`t.oob`).
- **Bombers:** blast craters fade after `CRATER_LIFE` s (nukes 4×). Bomb blasts use `explosion(..., {rs})` with rs 1.25 for player bombs and 0.8 for enemy bombs, so radius grows with the cube root of the charge (bigger bombs, bigger AOE). Bombers whose guns were turrets (B-17, B-29, Ju 88, He 111, He 177, Pe-8, Wellington, Lancaster, F.222) have `g:[]`: no forward guns, only AI gunners (`gn`, calibre `tg`); real fixed-gun bombers (B-25J, Il-2, Pe-2, Tu-2S, MB.175, LeO 451) keep theirs.
- **Living map (all generated from the seed, so guests match):** rivers/bridges (`collapseBridge`), mud, hills (+15% pen), forest hiding (`hiddenIn`), villages, barrels, minefields (`mineCheck`), a train (`trainUpd`) and road trucks (`trucksUpd`), weather/night (`pickWeather`, `spotted`), wave objectives hold/defend/escort (`objPick`, `objUpd`, `objEnd`; snapshot `ob`; aircraft count for holding). The top-middle event strip (`objHUD`, height `evH`) names the weather and objective. Wrecks fade 10 s after the kill or break up when shot or blasted (`wreckGone`). While dead, the status panel shows the teammate being watched. `store.noHull` turns off tank destroyers turning their hull toward the aim (`inp.nh`). Rocket planes: `aar` unguided air-to-air, `aam` homing missiles with lock-on (`lockUpd`, `lockHUD`).
- **Stalemate breaker:** `stallUpd`: with 3 or fewer enemies left, nothing queued and no enemy destroyed for 60 s, command sends AI hunters (`t.hunt`, `hunterAI`): a ground vehicle whose gun beats the target's front armour (`armourOf`, `penAt`), or two missile fighters for aircraft; up to 4 times a wave, 45 s apart (`lastKillT`, `huntN`, `huntT`).
- **Enemy AI:** tanks prefer targets they can penetrate and flank armor they can't (`aiCanPen`), retreat to repair when badly hurt (`A.retreat`), TDs reposition after firing, artillery leads movers and aims at clusters. Planes jink when chased or near AA, break off when overheated, solve lead iteratively, and bombers lead moving targets. AA picks the most dangerous aircraft (`airThreat`) and solves lead (`leadAim`). AI planes take 0.85× damage.
- **Outnumbered:** `swarmUpd` keeps `swarmK` (1–1.8) from enemy ground vehicles per ground vehicle on our side (from 2.5 each); our side takes `1/swarmK` damage (`hurt1`), reloads and repairs faster, and the HUD shows OUTNUMBERED +N%. Guests get it as `snap.sw`. `fireSlot` stops pile-ons: at most 3 enemy tank shots per 1.6 s (2 when `swarmK>1.3`) at the same target. Our side's AA hits planes 1.45× harder with a 1.3× fuze and 1.15× reach; our fighters' bullets do 1.25× vs planes and AI ally fighters also get `gunAssist`.
- **AI allies:** when a wave brings aircraft and nobody in the squad flies or drives AA, `spawnAllies` sends 1–3 AI fighters (one tier above the lead player where possible, 0.55× damage taken) plus an AA vehicle when 3+ planes, of the lead player's nation. Allies are team `p` with `t.ally`; enemy AI targets `targets()` (humans plus allies). They leave when the wave is cleared.
- **Admin vehicles:** ground: M1A2 Abrams, Object 279, FV4005, Char 2C, P.1000 Ratte; air: B-52D, Ho 229, Tu-95, Vulcan, Mirage IIIC. Tier 6 with `admin:1`, immortal, never spawned as enemies. Unlocked by the admin button (solo) or a redeem code (any mode).
- **Server:** codes, live settings (`LIVE`, `rpMul`, news bar, maintenance, `minBuild`) and badges come from the owner's private Cloudflare Worker (address in `web/codes.json`). Its source and how it works are deliberately kept out of this public repo: to change it, ask the owner for a temporary Cloudflare API token, download the live script from the Cloudflare API, edit, redeploy keeping its bindings (KV `USED`, secret `SECRET`, Durable Object `HUB` class `Hub`), test locally with `miniflare`, and keep using the token for the rest of that session (the owner wants it kept until they say otherwise). The server has three addresses: the Worker on workers.dev, a Deno Deploy app `ironfield-api` in the owner's `umvtt` organization (ironfield-api.umvtt.deno.net, a small `main.ts` that forwards every request and the live WebSocket to the Worker; deployed through api.deno.com/v2 with an organization `ddo_` token from the owner; it exists because Turkish ISPs block *.workers.dev and *.pages.dev by name), and a Cloudflare Pages project `ironfield-api` (ironfield-api.pages.dev, a `_worker.js` that forwards everything to the Worker through a service binding `API`). `web/codes.json` lists them (`api`, `alt`); the game (`codeAPIs`, `apiIdx`, `apiWorked`, `apiNext`) and the panel switch to the other address when one fails and remember the one that works. All data lives in the Hub Durable Object's SQLite storage (strongly consistent, high free limits); every response carries CORS headers, even errors. The panel retries network failures. Keep old requests working. Games keep a live WebSocket to it (`liveConnect`, `/live`) so saved settings arrive instantly (`applyLive`, battle notices via `liveNote`); badge callsigns are locked (`LIVE.names`, `nameOk`, `lockNames`, host `hostName`), compared by `nkey` (case, spaces, accents, repeated letters and look-alikes like 0/o, 1/l/i, 7/t don't make a new name). A verified badge holder always carries the server's callsign (`BADGE` value `nm`; the callsign box is read-only for them); a failed badge check never renames anyone. The panel's "New device code" makes a one-use code (`k:'badgeref'`) that gives an existing badge to another device. Badges carry abilities the owner toggles in the panel, and the holder can switch each one off in the hangar (`store.abOff`, sent as `hello.ao`, host keeps `pe.abOff`; `myAbAll` is the unfiltered set) (`badgeAb`, `myAb`, `peerAb`: `nuke` = nukes in co-op, `admin` = admin vehicles every session (`perkAll`, `peAll`; admin vehicles earn no RP in rooms), `kick` = can't be kicked, `vote` = starts the next wave alone); edits bump `LIVE.bv` so every game re-checks its badges. The owner's panel is an unlinked page in `web/`; keep it bland (no titles, explanations or comments) and never put the key, codes or explanations of the scheme in the repo.
- **Accounts and cloud saves:** `/acct` (`reg`, `login`, `load`, `save`, `out`, `pw`, `bt`) with PBKDF2 passwords and session tokens (`store.acct`). Signed in, `persist()` marks the save dirty and `cloudPush` uploads the 3 slots after 8 s; the server cuts back growth faster than a real player could earn (it keeps a per-vehicle cost table from `ROSTER`; regenerate it when vehicles change) and returns the corrected save (`adoptCloud`). The callsign is the account name unless a badge sets it.
- **Open rooms:** hosts with "show my room" (`store.pub`) check in every 20 s (`roomUp`, `/room`); the multiplayer tab lists `/rooms` (`roomsPoll`). The live socket sends `hi` (who/where, for the panel's live players) and `stat` (wave cleared, for daily stats), and handles `msg`, `kick`, `ban` (`store.ban` blocks multiplayer), `unban`, `save`. Hosts check new guests' device ids against bans (`/bchk`).
- **Connection test:** the hangar's Connection test button (and "Why?" after a failed code or sign-in) runs `runDiag`: codes.json, each server address (GET, POST, live socket), plain Cloudflare and jsDelivr, recent failures (`apiLog`), as a copyable report. News pops up once per news item (`newsPop`, `store.newsSeen`), goes in the battle log at the start of a battle, and reaches guests through the lobby (`nw`) when their own connection fails. Codes redeemed in the Multiplayer tab go to the slot picked in `#hostSave`.
- **Server relay:** a guest whose network can't reach the server sends `/redeem`, `/badge`, `/verify`, `/bchk` through the host (`RELAY`, `relayCall`, `api`/`apir`, never with the account token); the lobby carries the host-verified badges (`bl`) so badges show on every screen. RP and vehicle codes redeemed in a room also count in the room (host verifies the ticket, once per ticket).
- **Admin panel** (glass style, full screen): news/settings, live players (message, kick, ban), open rooms, bans, accounts (edit save slots, RP, vehicles, reset password, delete), stats (daily players, waves, codes, top vehicles), codes, redeem log, badges, security.
- **Versions:** `PROTO` is sent in `hello` and `lobby`; a host turns away guests on another version (`{e:'old'}`) and guests leave rooms run by another version. Bump `PROTO` whenever co-op messages change.
- **Multiplayer gate:** hosting/joining only works on `umvtt.github.io` (or localhost for tests) (`MP_OK`); downloaded copies play solo.
- **Waves:** handled by `startWave` and `pickEnemy` / `pickPlane` / `pickAA`. Wrecks clear when a wave is won. Milestone checkpoints are at waves 5, 10, 20 and so on. Between waves the squad votes to continue.
- **Host tools:** Kick (two clicks; the name is refused for the rest of the session via `net.banned`) and Lock room (`net.locked`) in the room view and the in-battle menu (`renderHostPanel`).
- **Co-op:** PeerJS star topology, with the host authoritative for up to 14 players. Peers live in `net.peers` and the host's view of all players is `humans()`. Snapshots (`sendSnapshot`) are per-guest JSON strings over the `raw` serialization. Every network input is clamped (`num`, `cleanName`, `cleanText`); never put remote text into innerHTML. The map size scales with player count (`mapDims`: 7200×4800 up to 10800×7200). The ground is painted lazily in 512 px tiles (`drawGround`, LRU of `GT_MAX`) with a small whole-map overview for zoomed-out views; never go back to one whole-map canvas (too big for phones). Obstacles are looked up through a grid (`inObs`, `obsGrid`); remove them only with `removeObs`. Enemies and planes spawn in a ring 1.3–2.4k px around the squad (`spawnPoint`, `spawnPlane`). The minimap shows a window around the player. Guests predict their own turret and muzzle flash locally (`guestUpdate`, `net.predShot`).
- **Connection:** the PeerJS library loads from jsDelivr, unpkg or cdnjs (`PEER_SRC`, first that works). PeerJS over public STUN only (no TURN relay yet: that needs a Cloudflare token with Realtime/Calls permission). A silent link counts as lost after 15 s. Guests reconnect by themselves (`rejoin`, up to 4 tries) and are hot-joined back into the battle; the host holds a dropped guest's RP, research and stats for 2 minutes (`holdGuest`, `net.away`, `bankAway`) before banking their RP. `MP_LOCK` is the owner's emergency switch that closes multiplayer in the game itself.
- **Saves:** localStorage key `ironfield-v4`, holding `{active, slots[3], coop, name, lastCode}` (`coop` is legacy and unused). The host plays on its active save slot. The host picks the room's save in the Host card (`#hostSave`: a slot, or a fresh start in an empty slot, or a session-only `net.temp`). Each guest plays on a copy of that save (`pe.rp` starts at the host's RP, `pe.mine`; `guestProfile(pe)` = the host's non-admin vehicles + starters + their own research), mirrored on the guest as `net.room` (from the lobby's per-guest `pf` and each snapshot's per-guest `pr`) and never written to the guest's storage. `rpFor` pays the host's save for the host and `pe.rp` for a guest. Guests research instantly on their own profile (`doResearch`) and the host repeats it (`guestResearch`). Only the host's own progress is kept: when a guest leaves, `bankGuest` just remembers their room RP and research by name (`net.left`) for a rejoin. Admin vehicles in a room still need a code (`owned` hides them).
- **Spectating:** while dead you watch a teammate (`viewer`, `specList`, `specIdx`), switching with A/D, arrows or tapping the screen edges.
- **What's new:** the publish workflow writes `version.json` with `notes` (commit title), `details` (the commit body's bullet list) and `build`; the hangar's "What's new" box shows it, expanded once per new version. So write commit bodies as short player-facing `- ` bullets.
- **Rules:** co-op has no nuke and no admin unlocks (except through badge abilities). The host can also sit out between waves (`hostBench`/`hostRejoin`): the battle keeps running on the host's device with the hangar shown over it. Guests can only leave during the break between waves (`benchMe` → host confirms with `benched`); they can rejoin any time from the hangar ("Join the battle" → `hello` → `hotJoin`, keeping their battle stats). Wave RP only goes to players on the field (`humans()`) when the wave is cleared. French vehicles explode randomly when the host's setting is on (off by default; `store.frOff` switched every old save off once).

## Working on it
- After edits, check the script parses: extract the `<script>` block and run `node --check` on it.
- Test in a headless browser (Playwright) on desktop and on a phone viewport (390×844 and 844×390), and check for page errors.
- Balance changes should be checked with quick simulations, not guesses.
- The owner speaks English (Turkish, lives in Turkey), plays on desktop and phone, and plays co-op with friends.
